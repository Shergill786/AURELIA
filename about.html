Our Story

Crafted for Every Wardrobe

Aurelia began with a simple idea — that quality clothing for men, women and kids shouldn't mean compromising on craft, comfort, or conscience.

Since 2018

A House Built on Fabric & Detail

What started as a small atelier making made-to-order jackets has grown into a full wardrobe destination — spanning tailored menswear, fluid womenswear, and playground-ready kidswear, all held to the same standard.

Every Aurelia piece passes through the hands of people who genuinely care about stitching, drape, and how a garment feels after the fiftieth wash — not just the first.
